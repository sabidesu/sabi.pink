i was out grocery shopping at costco today, and i was just about to check out when a costco employee approached me and asked if i was an executive member. i wasn’t, and though i had a gut feeling he was going to sell me a membership upgrade, i chose to hear him out anyways…*i’m also unfortunately too nice for my own good* 

they asked if i spent a lot at costco, which is an incredibly vague question. so naturally, i asked “how much is a lot?” apparently, over $3k USD a year! while i’d been shopping more often ever since i moved here (seattle) back in august, i didn’t think i’d been spending that much, but i wasn’t too sure. they offered to check my membership, to which i agreed, so they scanned my membership card, and went off for a little bit while i scanned my groceries

right as i finished up scanning, they came back and told me i was well under that, and that i actually didn’t need an executive membership right now. it was actually nice to hear, since i was considering upgrading for my increased grocery spending, but wasn’t sure how much it’d be worth it. 

in a world where it feels like everyone’s trying to sell you or upsell you something, it’s surprisingly relieving to hear a salesperson say “actually, you don’t need this right now! what you have is fine as is!”

there was also an older woman in the parking lot who reminded me about the soda i forgot to take out of my cart right after i put it in the stall. i laughed and thanked her while grabbing it. it’s easy to forget that people are generally nicer than you might think
